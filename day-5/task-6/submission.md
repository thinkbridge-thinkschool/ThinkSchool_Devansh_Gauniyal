## What did you learn this session?
I learned that a circuit breaker's failure ratio is meaningless without a separate MinimumThroughput setting -- Polly won't even evaluate the ratio until enough calls have happened in the window, which is why the production default of 100 makes it untestable without a much smaller test-only value.

## What would break this?
If `EnsureSuccessStatusCode()` were ever removed from `RemoteService`, a request that exhausts every retry would return the failed response's body as if it were a success, and the caller would have no way to know the data is actually missing.
